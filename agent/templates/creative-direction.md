# Creative direction

Write decisions, not adjectives. "Modern", "clean", "premium", "beautiful" and similar words are allowed only when followed by the visual decisions they mean (for example "premium: generous margins of 12vw, one serif display face at 96px, no more than two images per screen").

## Concept
One or two sentences: the idea that makes this site belong to this business.

## Audience and context
Who visits, on what device, in what situation, wanting what.

## Brand personality
Three to five traits, each with the visual consequence.

## Composition and layout
Grid, content width, asymmetry, section rhythm, how the hero is composed. How this differs from the obvious layout for the category.

## Typography direction
Families, why they fit, scale contrast, where each is used.

## Colour direction
Palette roles and why. Confirm no default purple or blue-purple (master prompt §3.1).

## Image and media direction
Subject, setting, light, colour treatment, crop behaviour, sources to try.

## Experience patterns
List every non-conventional pattern (cinematic hero, scroll narrative, pinned section, horizontal journey, parallax, product exploration, before/after, editorial transitions, 3D). Conventional static sections need no entry. Do not add a pattern only to look sophisticated.
