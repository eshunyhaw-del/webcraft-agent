# Delivery report

Write this in plain language for the owner (master prompt §9 and §13.4).

## What was built
Pages, features, and the main design and engineering decisions.

## How to see it
The exact command and address, or the file to open.

## How to change text and images
Where the copy and media live and how to edit them.

## How to put it online
The hosting route that fits the stack, step by step.

## What is not real yet
| Item | Kind (placeholder, unverified fact, prototype feature, unconnected integration) | What the owner needs to supply or decide |
|---|---|---|

## What was tested
Summary of the validation and visual QA reports: what passed, what failed and was fixed, and every check that was NOT RUN with its reason.

## Suggested next steps
One or two.
