    Go outside. Here is what you are looking at.

    The Moon is the easiest place to start. Find its phase for any date, then try these four habits that make the night sky far easier to enjoy.

    Next new Moon
    Next full Moon

    • Give your eyes twenty minutes

      Dark adaptation takes about 20 minutes. Fainter stars appear that you could not see when you first stepped outside.

    • Protect your night vision

      Phone screens ruin it in seconds. Turn the brightness down, or use a red light, which disturbs dark adaptation far less.

    • Choose the dark half of the month

      Faint things such as the Milky Way are easiest near a new Moon, when moonlight is not washing the sky out.

    • Start with binoculars

      A basic pair shows lunar craters, Jupiter's four largest moons and star clusters, and it is far easier to aim than a telescope.
