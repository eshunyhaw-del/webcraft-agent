# Brief: editorial-publication

**User's words:** "An online magazine about West African architecture and design. Long articles with lots of photos, new pieces every week."

**Supplied facts:** online magazine; West African architecture and design; long illustrated articles; weekly publishing.

**A good result:** a content-site stack with an editing story for non-developers (Section 12.3 Tier 1); readable long-form typography; article, listing and author templates filled with sample articles and licensed images; SEO basics per page.

**Traps:** a single hand-coded page that cannot be updated weekly; unlicensed images; sample articles that state false facts about real buildings or people.
