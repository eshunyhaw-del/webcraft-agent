# Brief: saas-product

**User's words:** "Landing page for our SaaS. It helps small accounting firms chase late client documents automatically. We want trial sign-ups."

**Supplied facts:** product purpose; audience small accounting firms; goal trial sign-ups.

**A good result:** a hero that says what it does and for whom in one read; how it works explained concretely; a sign-up CTA that goes to a real destination or is marked as needing one; pricing only as a placeholder.

**Traps:** the generic blue-purple SaaS gradient; fake customer logos, "10,000+ firms" counters, made-up pricing tiers, invented integrations.
